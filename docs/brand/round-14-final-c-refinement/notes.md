## Core Idea

**Suspended Continuation**

Chinese:

**未合，不等于终止。**

The detached lower element is not missing. It represents another layer, another thought, another continuation.

## Four Variants

### C-F1 - Minimal Optical Refinement

Keeps the current form almost unchanged. The main body is preserved; the detached lower element is moved slightly farther right and upward, with a small proportional tightening, so its relationship to the upper/lower transition feels more inevitable while staying suspended.

### C-F2 - More Suspended

Strengthens the lower piece so it feels more intentional and alive. The piece feels less like something that fell away and more like another state of the same thought.

### C-F3 - More Editorial

Softens the mechanical feeling and moves toward publication / archive / research mark. It is the calmest variant, but loses some emotional voltage.

### C-F4 - Hidden Layer

Strengthens the relationship between outer form, inner pause, and lower continuation. It has the strongest "visible layer is not the whole structure" story.

## Current Preference

### C-F1 - Minimal Optical Refinement

C-F1 is currently the preferred direction because the spatial relationship between the main body and the detached lower element carries the right emotional identity. The latest adjustment only refines the detached element's position, proportion, and optical balance: farther from the main body's left-side pull, closer to the transition area, still suspended.

## Previous Top 2

### C-F2 - More Suspended

- First glance: the lower piece catches attention without feeling decorative.
- Second glance: it feels like another state of the same thought.
- Brand story: Understory / 问溯 / One More Question: one answer is not the end.
- Strength: strongest memory and emotional attachment.
- Risk: still closest to the C/Q-adjacent source form.
- Long-term potential: high, because the suspended piece is distinctive.

### C-F4 - Hidden Layer

- First glance: compact, memorable, slightly mysterious.
- Second glance: the inner pause and lower element reveal a structure beneath.
- Brand story: the visible layer is not the whole structure.
- Strength: strongest conceptual connection to Understory.
- Risk: slightly less immediate than C-F2.
- Long-term potential: high, especially if the next pass refines optical spacing.

## Recommendation

C-F1 is the lead because the spatial relationship between the main body and detached lower element has the right emotional identity.

C-F4 is the strategic alternate if the priority is hidden-structure storytelling.

Do not choose the cleanest version by default. The unresolved relationship is the identity.
