# Understory Logo / Brand Symbol Round 2

Round 2 refines the first-round C direction, `Understory Question`, without changing the live site. The shared logic is an open, layered U-like structure that suggests surface and underlying structure, then leaves a small unresolved point rather than a literal question mark.

Existing visual system references:

- Brand green: `#174D3C`
- Deep green: `#0F352B`
- Page: `#F7F4EE`
- Surface: `#FFFDF8`
- Text: `#191714`
- Border: `#DED7CA`
- Current wordmark style: lowercase Georgia, restrained editorial tone

## C2-A — Nested Open U

C2-A keeps the mark at its most reduced: two nested open U structures with a small detached point at the lower right. The outer stroke reads as the visible surface; the inner stroke is the underlying layer discovered on the second look. The point is intentionally close to, but not part of, the U, suggesting an unfinished thought rather than a standard question mark. This is the most favicon-oriented version.

- Strength: Best small-size clarity and most restrained silhouette.
- Risk: May read as a quiet abstract U before the “one more question” idea is noticed.
- Recognizability: High as a compact brand symbol; moderate as a conceptual mark.
- Favicon performance: Strong at 32px and 24px; acceptable at 16px because the structure stays simple.

## C2-B — Descending Question

C2-B emphasizes inquiry as downward movement. The outer form remains stable and editorial, while the internal path drops below the surface, bends, and ends near a point. It hints at a question gesture without drawing a question mark. The result is more analytical and less monogram-like: surface, depth, unresolved question.

- Strength: Strongest embodiment of “go beneath the obvious.”
- Risk: The inner descending path can feel more intricate at favicon scale.
- Recognizability: Good in medium and large contexts; more subtle at 16px.
- Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, borderline at 16px unless optically simplified later.

## C2-C — Continuing Understory

C2-C keeps the layered U from the first-round C direction but lets a path continue outward beyond the internal structure. The mark suggests that analysis produces insight, then opens into the next question or future contribution layer. It is the most narrative of the three: not only depth, but continuation and expansion.

- Strength: Best bridge between “underlying structure” and “one more question.”
- Risk: Slightly more directional and active; needs careful spacing beside the wordmark.
- Recognizability: High at header and social sizes because the outward path creates a distinctive profile.
- Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, weaker at 16px due to the extended tail and dot.
