Understory / Logo Round 2

Go beneath. Ask again.

Second-round refinement of the first-round C direction. The goal is not a new visual territory, but three durable variations on the same brand logic: surface, underlying structure, and the small unresolved point that keeps analysis moving.

All marks are pure SVG, single-color capable, and tested here against the current site palette, Georgia wordmark, desktop/mobile headers, favicon sizes, and light application contexts.

A. Concept Board

Three refined C directions

Each mark is shown large, black on white, white on black, brand green on tint, dark brand field, favicon sizes, and paired with the current wordmark.

Design note: 最简洁的版本：双层开放 U 形成 surface / underneath，右下点是未完成的 insight，而非问号句点。结构紧凑、留白清楚，第一眼可作为 symbol，第二眼能读到内部层次，最适合作 favicon 与长期基础标识。

Strength: 小尺寸最稳，克制且耐用。

Risk: 追问意味较隐，需要品牌语境补足。

Recognizability: High.

Favicon performance: Strong at 32/24px; acceptable at 16px.

Descending Question

Design note: 外部 U 保持稳定，内部路径从表层向下沉，再以很轻的弯折靠近点位。它强调 surface → depth → unresolved question，不直接画问号。相比 A 更有分析动作，适合表达“往下挖”的方法论。

Strength: “go beneath the obvious” 概念最直接。

Risk: 16px 时内部动作会变得微妙。

Recognizability: Medium-high.

Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, borderline at 16px.

Continuing Understory

Design note: 延续第一轮 C 的层级 U，但让内部路径向右外延，最后靠近一个小点。它表达 analysis → insight → next question，也隐含未来 contribution layer：分析不是终点，而是开放系统中的下一次进入。

Strength: 叙事性最强，与 “one more question” 连接最好。

Risk: 横向尾部让 header 间距更敏感。

Recognizability: High at header/social sizes.

Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, weaker at 16px.
