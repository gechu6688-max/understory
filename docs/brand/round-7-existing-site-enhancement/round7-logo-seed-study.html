Understory / Round 7

Potential logo seeds from site behavior.

Strict boundary

These are not logos, not U/Q monograms, and not favicon studies. Each fragment is a primitive mechanism extracted from the Round 7 site experiments.

Seed fragments

Four to six primitive fragments, unresolved on purpose.

A future symbol should not be invented separately from the website. It should come from a repeated behavior readers already understand: reveal, interruption, evidence, offset, continuation, or second reading.

Seed 01

Exposed lower plane

Source mechanism
Experiment A / Beneath Reveal.
Where it appears
Quick Thesis lower layer; section divider with a visible analytical underside.
Why native
It expresses Understory's behavior: surface claim first, structure underneath second.
Could become symbol?
Yes. Strongest candidate, if kept more like a reveal cut than an app badge.

Seed 02

Interrupted editorial rule

Source mechanism
Experiment D / Editorial Refinement.
Where it appears
Source strip, case opener rules, section transitions.
Why native
The current site already relies on rules; interruption can become proprietary without changing the system.
Could become symbol?
Maybe. It is elegant, but may need more distinctiveness before symbol work.

Seed 03

Evidence notch

Source mechanism
Experiment B / Evidence Annotation.
Where it appears
Evidence, counterargument, judgement, and one-more-question markers.
Why native
Understory's method is evidence-led; a notch can mark a claim that has been checked.
Could become symbol?
Possibly. Better as a system mark than as the main brand symbol.

Seed 04

Offset second layer

Source mechanism
Experiment A plus Experiment C.
Where it appears
Claim followed by lower structure; first reading followed by complication.
Why native
It turns "ask again" into alignment behavior rather than punctuation.
Could become symbol?
Yes, but it risks becoming too diagrammatic if overbuilt.

Seed 05

Margin continuation

Source mechanism
Experiment B / Evidence Annotation.
Where it appears
Selective margin-side method markers and future contribution cues.
Why native
It suggests analysis continuing beyond a single paragraph or answer.
Could become symbol?
Maybe later. It is more useful as a contribution-layer device for now.

Seed 06

Claim / counterclaim crossing

Source mechanism
Experiment C / Second Reading.
Where it appears
Paired first conclusion and second complication.
Why native
It visualizes a serious alternative explanation intersecting the initial claim.
Could become symbol?
Low priority. Conceptually strong, but more likely to feel invented than site-born.
