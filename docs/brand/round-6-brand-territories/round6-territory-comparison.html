Understory / Round 6

Brand territory before symbol.

Phase one contains no logo design. This board explores the visual worlds that could make Understory feel ownable before a future symbol emerges naturally from the system.

All territories use the existing Understory content language: cases, evidence, counterargument, judgement, hidden costs, and one more question.

A. Diagnostic

Why Rounds 1-5 did not fully land

The previous work improved craft, but it stayed inside symbol semantics rather than defining a full editorial system.

Symbol-first thinking: Most rounds began with U, Q, tails, hidden U, or layer geometry before a broader visual world existed.

Construction over identity: Many outputs were variations of a compact glyph container rather than a system for cases, evidence, counterargument, and long-form reading.

Favicon pressure too early: Small-size optimization encouraged thick closed silhouettes and app/SaaS associations before the brand territory was mature.
