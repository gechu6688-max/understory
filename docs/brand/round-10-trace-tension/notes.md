# Understory Round 10 - Trace & Tension Refinement

Round 10 responds to the user's stated preference after Round 9: B2 Embedded Trace and C3 Unresolved Join feel more compelling than A3, even though A3 scored highest on mature brand presence.

This round therefore optimizes for attraction, story, tension, memory, and genuine attachment rather than maturity alone.

No live site files were modified. The current wordmark, typography, grid, navigation, and palette remain unchanged.

## Round 9 Preference Read

- B2's strength: hidden story, trace, discovery, 问溯, one question leading to another.
- B2's weakness: the mark can feel too rational, too slab-like, or too close to an E/folder/maze if not refined.
- C3's strength: emotional tension, incompletion, continuation, the sense that one answer is not the whole story.
- C3's weakness: the separated element can feel constructed, fallen off, or close to a broken Q/gaming emblem.

## Phase 1 Candidates

### B1 - Deeper Embedded Trace

Goal: make the trace more discovered and less literal.

- Strength: preserves a strong outer silhouette and reduces the E/folder feeling compared with Round 9 B2.
- Risk: still quite rational; emotional pull is moderate.
- Best role: conservative B-family refinement.

### B2 - Interrupted Embedded Trace

Goal: make the thread disappear and reappear without becoming a route map.

- Strength: strongest hidden-thread story in the B family.
- Risk: must avoid becoming maze/circuit-like if more detail is added.
- Best role: strongest pure trace direction.

### B3 - Returning Trace

Goal: suggest a path returning inward.

- Strength: good conceptual fit with 问溯.
- Risk: return logic can become too explained and less visually immediate.
- Best role: useful study, not a lead.

### C1 - Integrated Missing Piece

Goal: make the separated element feel related rather than fallen off.

- Strength: reduces the broken-piece problem.
- Risk: still not as memorable as C3.
- Best role: structural correction study.

### C2 - Offset Completion

Goal: show the missing part exists elsewhere.

- Strength: direct continuation idea.
- Risk: can drift toward UI-card/offset construction.
- Best role: reject as lead unless heavily simplified.

### C3 - Tension Gap

Goal: remove detached-piece dependency and make the gap itself carry tension.

- Strength: strongest emotional tension.
- Risk: still needs optical refinement to feel inevitable rather than assembled.
- Best role: lead tension direction.

### H1 - Hidden Trace + Unresolved Join

Goal: combine B2's hidden trace and C3's unresolved tension without geometric stacking.

- Strength: best bridge between story and tension; strongest genuine-attachment potential.
- Risk: needs simplification and optical correction so the hybrid does not become too busy.
- Best role: lead candidate for next refinement.
