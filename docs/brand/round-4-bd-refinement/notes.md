# Understory Round 4 — B / D Refinement Study

Round 4 narrows the field to the two strongest Round 3 families:

- R3-B: strongest conceptual connection through hidden negative-space structure.
- R3-D: strongest standalone brand presence, compact silhouette, and favicon performance.

The working hypothesis is that a future final direction may combine D’s compact memorability with B’s discovered negative-space logic. This round does not install or modify the live site.

## Family B — Hidden U Inside Q Refinement

### B1 — Open Q

Concept: Keeps the Q-like mass but introduces a side opening so it stops feeling like a closed badge or app button. The U remains internal and discoverable rather than explicit.

- Strength: Reduces the sealed-circle feeling while preserving Q recognition.
- Risk: The opening can make the mark less compact than R3-B.
- Brand distinctiveness: Medium-high.
- Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, moderate at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: Moderate-high.

### B2 — Offset U

Concept: Keeps the outer Q compact but optically shifts the internal U so it feels found in the structure instead of centered as an inserted letter.

- Strength: Quietly removes institutional symmetry.
- Risk: Still close to “U inside Q” if not pushed further.
- Brand distinctiveness: Medium.
- Favicon performance: Good at 32px and 24px, moderate at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: Moderate.

### B3 — Asymmetric Q

Concept: Moves away from a perfect-circle badge by stretching and weighting the outer Q into a more editorial silhouette.

- Strength: Best B-family correction against badge / seal associations.
- Risk: Slightly less stable in very small sizes.
- Brand distinctiveness: High.
- Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, moderate at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: High.

## Family D — Interlocking U/Q Refinement

### D1 — Softer Interlock

Concept: Preserves D’s compact silhouette but rounds the body and lets the tail grow from the shared curve rather than feeling attached.

- Strength: Best correction against rigid tech-square feeling.
- Risk: May lose some of R3-D’s crisp small-size snap.
- Brand distinctiveness: Medium-high.
- Favicon performance: Strong at 32px and 24px, acceptable at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: High.

### D2 — Negative-Space Interlock

Concept: Reduces explicit letter drawing by making the U emerge through an internal channel within one compact symbol.

- Strength: Strongest D-family bridge toward B’s hidden-structure logic.
- Risk: Internal U may become too readable if spacing is widened.
- Brand distinctiveness: High.
- Favicon performance: Strong at 32px and 24px, acceptable at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: High.

### D3 — Open Corner Interlock

Concept: Keeps the compact interlock but opens an outer corner to imply continuation and “ask again” without using a question mark.

- Strength: Adds Understory’s unresolved quality while retaining recognizability.
- Risk: The open corner may look like a construction detail if not optically balanced.
- Brand distinctiveness: Medium-high.
- Favicon performance: Strong at 32px, good at 24px, acceptable at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: Medium-high.

## Hybrid Studies

### H1 — D Silhouette + B Negative Space

Concept: Starts from D’s compact container and uses B-like negative space to hide the U more subtly inside a shared Q body.

- Strength: Most promising convergence of compact silhouette and discovered structure.
- Risk: Still needs tuning so the negative space feels less like a literal U.
- Brand distinctiveness: High.
- Favicon performance: Strong at 32px and 24px, acceptable at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: High.

### H2 — Editorial U/Q Hybrid

Concept: A less rigid, more publishing-adjacent hybrid: asymmetric body, softer Q logic, and a discovered internal U without perfect-circle or square-icon associations.

- Strength: Best editorial temperament; feels less like a tech app.
- Risk: Slightly more complex silhouette than H1 or D2.
- Brand distinctiveness: High.
- Favicon performance: Good at 32px, acceptable at 24px, moderate at 16px.
- Real-brand-before-concept: High.

## Primary Evaluation

1. H1 — best convergence candidate.
2. D2 — best D-family refinement toward hidden-structure logic.
3. B3 — best B-family correction toward editorial distinction.
