# Understory Round 13 - The Question Beneath

Round 13 is a new focused exploration that reinterprets the suspended C lineage as:

**A question that goes deeper.**

It does not install a logo, modify the live site, redesign typography, or delete prior rounds.

## Source References

- Round 9 C3: raw unresolved join and emotional tension.
- Round 11 C11-B: best controlled suspended continuation.
- Round 12 B2: strongest second-state logic beyond the C shape.

The central constraint was to avoid normal question marks, Q letters, U/Q monograms, search/chat/AI symbols, and anything that reads as an education or FAQ icon.

## Phase 1 Families

### A - Descending Question

The question is expressed as downward movement. A2 is the cleanest study of a question continuing below the visible structure, but this family risks becoming tail-driven and therefore closer to Q logic than desired.

### B - Question Without The Dot

This family keeps the separated element from C11-B, but reinterprets it as continuation rather than punctuation. B2 is the strongest in the family because the lower state feels like the next thought instead of a missing dot.

### C - Hidden Question

This family best connects to Understory's "beneath the obvious" concept. C1 is strongest because the question is not drawn directly; it appears through counterform, opening, and the relationship between main body and lower state.

### D - Q + Suspended Piece

This family preserves the emotional tension of C11-B most directly. D2 is strongest because the detached piece feels inevitable, active, and memorable at small size.

### E - Editorial Question Mark

This family pushes the direction toward publication, archive, and research identity rather than app icon. E2 is strongest because it feels mature and least software-like, although it is quieter than D2.

## Top 3 Finalists

### 1. C1 - Hidden Counterform

- Concept: a question beneath the visible symbol, carried by negative space and inner movement.
- Strength: strongest conceptual match to Understory and 问溯.
- Risk: may be too subtle until repeated in brand use.
- Brand distinctiveness: high.
- Favicon performance: strong at 24/32 px; 16 px keeps silhouette but loses hidden logic.
- Real brand before concept: yes.

### 2. D2 - Inevitable Piece

- Concept: the separated piece becomes the next question, not a missing dot.
- Strength: strongest emotional tension and memorability.
- Risk: remains closest to C/Q adjacency.
- Brand distinctiveness: high.
- Favicon performance: best of the finalists.
- Real brand before concept: yes.

### 3. E2 - Archive Question

- Concept: a mature editorial mark where the question feels archived within the structure.
- Strength: best publication/research fit and least app-like.
- Risk: less surprising and less emotionally alive than D2.
- Brand distinctiveness: medium-high.
- Favicon performance: good at 32 px, acceptable at 24 px, compressed at 16 px.
- Real brand before concept: mostly yes.

## Recommendation

Do not pick a final logo yet.

The next useful refinement would be a narrow convergence study between:

- C1's hidden-question intelligence,
- D2's suspended emotional tension,
- E2's editorial maturity.

The key question for the next pass should be:

**Can the mark feel as memorable as D2, as conceptually Understory as C1, and as editorially mature as E2?**
