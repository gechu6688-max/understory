understory icon concepts

Three selectable directions for the Understory symbol. These are exploration assets only; the website currently uses the lowercase text wordmark until one direction is chosen.

Candidate A

Layered Understory

understory
Cases  Topics  Method
understory
Menu

Rationale: offset U-like layers create a surface/underlayer relationship.

Represents: what matters is not always what appears first.

Strengths: architectural, analytical, distinctive in monochrome.

Risk: could read as a monogram before the concept is explained.

Source: docs/brand/icons/icon-a-layered-understory.svg

Candidate B

Continuing Q

understory
Cases  Topics  Method
understory
Menu

Rationale: the Q tail continues into a second point instead of stopping.

Represents: one question leads to the next question.

Strengths: closest to the founder's One More Question idea.

Risk: if pushed too far, it may feel like a help/support icon.

Source: docs/brand/icons/icon-b-continuing-q.svg

Candidate C

Understory Question

Rationale: an open layered U resolves into a purposeful next-question point.

Represents: depth plus continuation in one compact mark.

Risk: slightly more complex at favicon size than A or B.
