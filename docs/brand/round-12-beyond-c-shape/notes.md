# Understory Round 12 - Beyond The C Shape

Round 12 is a focused refinement from Round 9 C3 and Round 11 C11-B. It does not start a new exploration and does not remove the detached relationship.

No live site files were modified. The current wordmark, typography, grid, navigation, and palette remain unchanged.

## Source Of Truth

- Round 9 C3 - Unresolved Join: raw emotional tension.
- Round 11 C11-B - Suspended Piece: best controlled suspended continuation.

Core idea:

**未合，不等于终止。**

The visible structure is not the whole structure. A thought remains open because one answer is never the whole story.

## Main Problem

The current direction is emotionally strong but too easily read as:

- C
- Q
- open circle
- broken emblem

Round 12 asks how to keep the suspended relationship while making the mark feel like a unique Understory symbol rather than a meaningful C-like symbol.

## Phase 1 Directions

### Direction A - Break The Circular Logic

- A1 Asymmetric Turn: reduces circularity through a sharper upper mass.
- A2 Weight Shift: changes the mass distribution, but loses some attraction.
- A3 Unexpected Shoulder: strongest A candidate; the upper shoulder interrupts the C expectation.

### Direction B - One Structure, Two States

- B1 Displaced State: promising, but still close to C11-B.
- B2 Second State: strongest overall. Detached piece feels like the same structure in another state.
- B3 Shared Angle: good structural logic, but too close to puzzle-fit reasoning.

### Direction C - Hidden Counterform

- C1 Inner Echo: strongest story depth, but still C/Q-adjacent.
- C2 Hidden Axis: reduces letter feeling but weakens emotional attraction.
- C3 Counterweight: useful balance study, but not a lead.

### Direction D - Editorial Symbol

- D1 Editorial Notch: less C-like but too rectangular/generic.
- D2 Chapter Mark: strongest editorial/publication energy.
- D3 Archive Signal: good archive feel, but less emotionally alive.

## Top 3 Finalists

### Finalist 1 - B2 Second State

- Best move beyond the C shape.
- Keeps the detached relationship but frames it as a second state of the same structure.
- Reduces C/Q reading more than C11-B.
- Best long-term candidate if refined optically.

### Finalist 2 - A3 Unexpected Shoulder

- Strongest structural turn.
- Interrupts the C expectation without abandoning suspended continuation.
- Good bridge direction, but less emotionally precise than B2.

### Finalist 3 - C11-B Suspended Piece

- Remains the emotional benchmark.
- Still strongest at "悬而未止."
- Weakness remains: C/Q reading is not solved.

## Final Evaluation

### Does this feel like Understory without explanation?

B2 and C11-B both do. B2 feels less letter-bound; C11-B feels more emotionally direct.

### Does it feel personal rather than generic?

C11-B still feels most personal because the suspended piece carries more emotional tension. B2 is close and may surpass it after refinement.

### Does the story deepen the mark?

Yes for B2: the "second state" story makes the detached relationship more meaningful without needing a long explanation.

### Would this still feel relevant 10 years later?

B2 has the strongest long-term potential because it is less dependent on C/Q recognition and more like a proprietary mark.

### Is it recognizable without the wordmark?

B2 first, C11-B second, A3 third.

## Recommendation

Move forward with B2 Second State as the lead for the next refinement round.

Keep C11-B visible as the emotional reference. The next round should not create new directions; it should make B2 more optically refined, more human, and more emotionally suspended, while checking it against C11-B so the tension is not lost.
